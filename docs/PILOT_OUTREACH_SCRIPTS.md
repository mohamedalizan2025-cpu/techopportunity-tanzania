# Pilot outreach scripts (copy/paste, keep human and short)

Rules for all messages: plain language, no hype, no payment promises
beyond what is stated, no personal data beyond what the pilot sheets
record. Swahili-friendly wording is fine where the recipient prefers
it — meaning first, never wordplay.

## 1. Participant recruitment (students, incl. secondary)

> Hi! I'm testing Tech Opportunity — a Tanzanian website for finding
> scholarships, internships and similar openings. I need 5–10 testers
> (students especially welcome, no experience needed). It takes about
> 15 minutes on your own phone, plus two short follow-up messages
> later. You just try the site while I watch and take notes — nothing
> is graded. Interested? Tell me a good time this week.

## 2. Participant recruitment (graduates / young professionals)

> Hi! I'm running 15-minute observed tests of Tech Opportunity (a
> site for verified Tanzanian opportunities) and looking for a few
> graduates/young professionals. Your own phone, your own goals, two
> short follow-ups after. Interested? Tell me a good time this week.

## 3. Seven-day follow-up (participants)

> Hi, quick follow-up on Tech Opportunity: did you open it (or a
> source page) again since the test? Save anything else, or start an
> application? What stopped you, if anything? Asante!

## 4. Two-week follow-up (participants, closes the pilot)

> Hi, last check-in! In this second week: did you visit Tech
> Opportunity again? Did any application progress change? Would you
> recommend it to a classmate — and did you? Thank you, this closes
> the test!

## 5. Provider first outreach (one provider only, after user pilot passes)

> Hello [Name/Organization]! We run Tech Opportunity, helping
> Tanzanian students and young professionals find verified openings.
> We'd like to offer you a FREE 2-week trial: we verify and feature
> your current open call, share it with one student community we can
> reach, then send aggregate engagement such as Saved, Interested,
> Applying and Applied, where available — never personal data.
> No cost, no obligation. Open to a 15-minute call?

## 6. Provider renewal nudge (after the trial report is delivered)

> Thanks for trying the pilot! Quick question now that you've seen
> the report: would a repeat run at [scoped price] be worth it for
> you? A yes or no with one reason helps us enormously — either
> answer is fine.
