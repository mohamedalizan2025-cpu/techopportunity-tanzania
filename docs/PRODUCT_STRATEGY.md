## Problem, audience and positioning

Working problem hypothesis: Tanzania's emerging talent loses time deciding which
opportunities are credible, accessible and worth the effort, then loses track
between discovery and applying. More links alone do not resolve that uncertainty.
Interviews and observed repeat use must still validate its frequency and severity.

Primary wedge: university students and recent graduates, initially through a
small campus/developer community. Secondary: early-career developers and other
professionals. Researchers and founders remain supported audiences but require
different supply and application journeys; do not launch six acquisition motions.
Tanzania first; African expansion is a hypothesis, not existing coverage proof.

## Jobs to be done

1. Discover: find study, work and innovation calls in one place without
   searching scattered groups and boards.
2. Verify / understand: check the source, deadline state and what is still
   unknown before spending effort.
3. Prioritize: see which listings fit an optional level, field, sector or
   opportunity-type profile, with a plain reason per suggestion.
4. Track: keep private Saved bookmarks separate from self-reported
   Interested / Applying / Applied progress.
5. Apply: leave the platform for the authoritative source to submit; the
   platform records progress but never submits on the user's behalf.

Positioning: an opportunity-intelligence and action platform for Tanzania's
emerging talent. Public promise: **Opportunities worth acting on.** Explain the
mechanism: inspect source evidence, prioritize with an optional profile, and keep
private bookmarks and application progress. Use **CareerTech / opportunity
intelligence** for competitions; EdTech is adjacent through education-to-career
access, not a claim to teach or improve learning outcomes. Talent infrastructure
is the longer-term business direction, not today's delivered institutional system.

Table stakes: mobile browsing, useful filters, source links, clear dates, saving,
tracking, accessible forms and understandable errors. The credible gap to TEST is
an accountable Tanzania-focused evidence-to-action service across opportunity
types. It is not yet an owned market position. Never claim “first”, “only”,
“largest”, guaranteed eligibility, guaranteed selection, superior AI or verified
partnerships without evidence. Accumulated local evidence and repeat community
distribution could become defensible; neither is an established moat today.

## Why Tech Opportunity instead of ChatGPT?

“ChatGPT is useful for researching and preparing an application. Tech Opportunity
is built to maintain a shared opportunity record for Tanzanian talent: source
evidence, human publication decisions, deadlines, explainable relevance and your
private application progress in one workflow. Our advantage has to be the quality
and continuity of that service. The model is replaceable; local trust and useful
distribution must be earned.”

Present this as the product thesis, followed by a real evidence-backed listing.
Current legacy corpus gaps prevent a blanket superiority claim. Compare equal
tasks, source accuracy and repeat use, not artificial AI benchmarks.

## Three-sided business and first revenue experiment

**Talent:** keep core discovery free. TZS 1,000/month Talent Plus is a later price
hypothesis only, conditional on repeated use and a premium job such as dependable
reminders/readiness support. No payment implementation or commitment now.

**Providers:** first plausible charge is a staff-managed, time-bounded campaign
service for one verified opportunity: evidence review, agreed community
distribution, and a manual aggregate report. Charge for work and delivery, never
for passing verification. Verified publication alone is insufficient evidence of
value. No guaranteed applicant counts; no claim the current pilot automates
targeted sending or attributes conversions.

**Institutions:** test assisted distribution and a staff-readable engagement
summary with one campus/hub. An annual service/dashboard/integration hypothesis
comes only after staff demonstrate repeated use and a budget owner identifies
value. Outcomes need consent and confirmation, not an “Applied” counter.

Before charging: one reachable consenting community, a timely useful call,
measured distribution effort/cost, a provider-approved delivery brief, reliable
count definitions, and a buyer willing to pay for the next run. Document actual
agreement; verbal enthusiasm is not willingness to pay. Review small-cohort
suppression before any external report; integer aggregates alone do not eliminate
re-identification. Existing analytics remain internal. Paid distribution must be
clearly labeled Sponsored and must never change verification, eligibility rules
or the completeness of Explore. Private talent data is never sold. Introductions
require specific consent and remain unbuilt.

First pilot proposal (not executed): one provider, one campus/developer community,
one independently reviewed open call, two weeks, free discovery. Establish the
provider's existing distribution baseline. Observe 5–10 consenting talent testers
finding evidence, deciding relevance and tracking truthful progress; follow up at
seven days. Record reach only when actually measured, return visits, source visits
where obtainable without new tracking, self-reported application progress,
provider feedback and staff hours. Existing counters are overlapping state
snapshots, not a sequential conversion funnel or causal impact measure. End with
a real scoped price proposal and a renewal decision; no invented price acceptance.

## Validated vs unvalidated assumptions

Validated in code or by direct inspection: human publication gating exists;
deterministic profile matching with reasons works; Saved and Interested /
Applying / Applied states are implemented with owner-only privacy; source,
deadline and eligibility evidence fields exist; legacy corpus contains stale
and geography-mismatched records; six listings were visible on 2026-10-02.

Unvalidated: that talent loses track often enough to return weekly; that
evidence presentation changes application decisions; that a campus community
will distribute through us; that a provider will pay for a managed campaign;
that TZS 1,000/month Talent Plus has a premium job worth paying for; that
aggregate engagement reports influence provider renewal; that Fursa AI,
WhatsApp groups or boards leave a gap users will switch for.

## UX audit and implementation plan (written before UI changes)

Keep the existing green identity and Geist; use a restrained ink/paper palette,
clear rules, smaller corner radii and denser typography. Local relevance should
come from language, geography and useful evidence, not decorative national motifs.

1. Replace generic tech-only hero with emerging-talent/action positioning and a
   compact source/profile/progress explanation. Keep search and useful records
   close to the top; collapse secondary highlights instead of duplicating the
   whole opportunity inventory above results. Avoid category availability claims.
2. Preserve Explore / For You / Activity. Expose those destinations anonymously
   (existing login redirects apply), show active navigation and move crowded
   authenticated navigation to a menu until laptop width. Preserve Saved/Profile.
3. Improve card scan order: type, title, organizer/source domain, short summary,
   deadline/location, evidence status, actions. Do not badge all records verified.
   Render eligibility only from stored decisions AND supporting evidence; retain
   unknown when trust fields are unavailable. Keep location separate from access.
4. Detail: expose canonical evidence separately from discovery/source links;
   explain verified badge scope and recorded versus checked dates. Do not derive
   new facts from prose or change trust admission. Keep Save/Activity behavior.
5. Align public forms, login, For You, Profile, Activity and loading surfaces with
   shared design primitives. Replace implementation vocabulary (“corpus”, etc.)
   with user-facing language. Keep errors actionable and focus visible.
6. Verify existing full contract + build, then browser QA at 360, 412, 768, 1366
   and 1920 widths, long titles, missing metadata, empty states, keyboard controls
   and authenticated/anonymous surfaces. Report any untested state explicitly.

Observed weaknesses: generic hero; extensive empty space before records; many
equally weighted category/buttons/cards; no active main navigation; tablet staff
navigation can overrun its space; repeated unknown eligibility hides existing
evidence; generic “Source page available” loses provenance; dark hero accent has
poor contrast; dated operational docs overstate trust and freshness. Existing
strengths: skip link, real forms, reduced-motion handling, query-preserving filters,
empty/error components, progressive profile and no required onboarding.

Boundary: no schema, RLS, migrations, data mutations, Discovery/Cloudflare changes,
AI activation, payments, provider self-service or institutional dashboard work.
No production deployment is included. A local verification result is not online
proof of the new UI. Existing source truth and product architecture are preserved.
