## Supply gaps vs the 2-week pilot

After cleanup (2 kept) + full shortlist approval (11), the shelf holds ~13
records — workable for 5–10 testers over two weeks, but thin and skewed:
heavy on fellowships/competitions, weak on jobs, internships, scholarships,
and tech events/hackathons, with exactly ONE Tanzania-based record
(UONGOZI) and zero student-scholarship anchors. Structural gaps:

1. National supply: the UDSM/NM-AIST adapters surface stale 2025 calls and
   selection notices; no fresh Tanzania-based call was found in the newest 80.
2. Source concentration: nearly all fresh volume is OpportunityDesk RSS
   reposts (secondary leads) — first-party evidence must still be attached
   per record at review time.
3. Backlog vs throughput: 238 pending is a large owner-review backlog;
   lifecycle expiry will weed the past-dated share, but approvals need
   bounded batches (5–8/week) or the queue keeps outrunning moderation.
4. Deadline cliff: the two strongest records (AfDB, MWF) close 12–13 Oct —
   if approvals slip a week, the shelf loses its flagships immediately.

Verdict: YES, the corpus can support the pilot — but only if (a) the 4
unpublishes execute, (b) the 11-record shortlist is approved in
deadline order starting this week, and (c) testers are briefed that supply
is fellowship-heavy. National sourcing (fresh UDSM/NM-AIST/campus calls) is
the next supply milestone, not a pilot blocker.
